DRAFT UPLOADER

A private upload helper

This is a small private tool of the video project behind Fino Asks and The Quiet Wire. It uploads short videos that the project produced itself to the project's own TikTok accounts as drafts. Nothing is posted automatically: every draft is reviewed, edited and published (or deleted) inside the TikTok app.

The tool is not offered to anyone else and has no public sign-up.
